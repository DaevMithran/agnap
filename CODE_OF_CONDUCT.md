# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in AGNAP welcoming, respectful, and
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience,
or level of technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and tradeoffs with clarity and respect.
- Critique specifications and implementations, not people.
- Give contributors room to ask questions and revise proposals.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Handle security reports and sensitive information responsibly.
- Accept maintainer decisions gracefully, including decisions not to adopt a
  proposal.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct,
doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private
information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban a participant when behavior is harmful to the
community.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer using the
contact information on the
[@daevmithran GitHub profile](https://github.com/daevmithran). Do not include
sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed promptly
and handled as confidentially as practical.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and whenever someone is publicly
representing AGNAP.
